# Capitalization, punctuation, numbers, abbreviations, figures and tables

## 1. Capitalization
- Capitalize only the first letter of a sentence and proper nouns. Do not capitalize ordinary words mid-sentence: "Mô hình Ngôn ngữ Lớn", "Bộ Điều Phối", "Worker", "Hệ Thống" are wrong.
- Names of agencies and organizations: capitalize the first letter of the first word and of the components that are proper names: "Trường Đại học Bách khoa Hà Nội", "Bộ Giáo dục và Đào tạo", "Khoa Công nghệ thông tin", "Phòng Đào tạo". Follow the official name if the organization has fixed one.
- "Trường", "Khoa", "Bộ" are capitalized when standing for a specific named body ("theo quy định của Trường"); lowercase when general ("các trường đại học").
- Names of legal documents: "Thông tư số 23/2021/TT-BGDĐT", "Quy chế đào tạo trình độ thạc sĩ".
- Section headings, figure/table captions, column headings: sentence case (capital for the first letter and proper nouns). No English Title Case ("Kiến Trúc Hệ Thống" → "Kiến trúc hệ thống").
- Chapter headings in all caps if the formatting template requires it: let the template uppercase them; do not type capitals in the source (this keeps the table of contents and search working).
- After a colon: lowercase, except for proper nouns, a complete quoted sentence, or list items that are full sentences on new lines.
- English terms kept as-is use their original form: gRPC, OpenSearch, iOS, macOS, JavaScript; ordinary words such as pipeline, worker are lowercase.

## 2. Punctuation and spacing
- No space before a period, comma, colon, semicolon or question mark; one space after.
- Parentheses and quotation marks: no space on the inside: "(ví dụ)", “trích”.
- Hyphen (-) in compounds: "Việt-Anh", "đa-tác-tử" (do not use if unnecessary); en dash (–) for ranges: "1–5", "2020–2025"; em dash (—) used sparingly in Vietnamese academic text.
- Quotation marks use “ ” (LaTeX: ``…'' or the csquotes package \enquote{…}).
- No "…", "!!!", ":)" in the main text.
- Lists: be consistent within one list. Items that are complete sentences: capitalized, ending with a period. Items that are phrases: lowercase, no period (or semicolons, with a period on the last item). Do not mix the two styles in one list.

## 3. Numbers and units (Vietnamese)
- Decimal separator is a comma: 4,95. Thousands separator is a period: 1.250; years, codes and identifiers have no separator: 2026, run 20250310.
- A number with a unit has one space between them: 512 MB, 3,2 giây, 90 °C; percent is attached: 99,0%.
- Numbers below 10 in running text may be written as words ("ba nhánh"), but stay consistent; measurements are always digits.
- Do not start a sentence with a digit: "1.250 câu hỏi được…" → "Bộ dữ liệu gồm 1.250 câu hỏi…".
- Ranges: "từ 1 đến 5" or "1–5"; not "1-5" (hyphen).
- Round consistently (the same number of decimal places within one table).

## 4. Abbreviations
- Define at first occurrence in the main text: "mô hình ngôn ngữ lớn (LLM)"; or the full English form if it is an English abbreviation: "sinh có truy xuất (Retrieval-Augmented Generation – RAG)".
- Include a list of abbreviations if the document is long; the list contains only terms that actually appear, in alphabetical order.
- No abbreviations in chapter/section headings or in the abstract (except very common ones such as AI, API).
- Do not coin abbreviations for phrases used only once or twice.

## 5. Figures and tables
- Every figure/table must be referred to in the main text before or right after it appears ("Hình 3.2 mô tả…"), and the text says what the reader should see, not just "như hình dưới".
- Table captions go above the table; figure captions go below the figure (per most Vietnamese school rules; check the school's template).
- Captions in sentence case, short, stating the content; give the source at the end of the caption if taken from another document ("Nguồn: [12]").
- Text in figures is legible when printed on A4 (equivalent to 8–9 pt or larger); terms in figures match terms in the main text.
- Tables: column headings in sentence case, units in the column heading ("Độ trễ (ms)"), numbers right-aligned or aligned on the decimal point, no heavy vertical rules (booktabs).
