# Thesis defense and academic presentations

Related: `phan-bien-khoa-hoc` (how reviewers read your work), `bao-cao-ket-qua` (figures and numbers), `van-phong-hoc-thuat`.

## 1. Rules for master's in Vietnam (Thông tư 23/2021/TT-BGDĐT; school rules add detail)
| | Luận văn (research track, Điều 9) | Đề án (applied track, Điều 11) |
|---|---|---|
| Council | ≥ 5 members: chair, secretary, 2 reviewers (≥ 1 external), members | ≥ 3 members: chair, secretary, reviewer |
| Supervisor | may sit on the council, does not score | per school rule |
| Quorum | ≥ 2/3 present incl. chair, secretary, ≥ 1 reviewer | per school rule |
| Score | mean of members' scores, 10-point scale; pass ≥ 5,5 | pass ≥ 5,5 (school rules may add: no member below 5,0 [unverified]) |
| Failing | revise and defend again within 3 months; no third time | second assessment within 45 days |
| Online | allowed with consent, must be recorded | |
| After passing | revised thesis lodged at the library and posted on the school site ≥ 30 days | |

Schools fix the detail: presentation length (one procedure: at most 20 minutes), revision deadline (examples: 30 days), forms. Read your school's defense procedure.

Typical session order: secretary reads the decision and candidate's file → candidate presents → reviewers read their reviews → members' questions → candidate answers → closed scoring session → announcement and minutes (biên bản) with required revisions.

## 2. Slide deck (15–20 minute talk ≈ 15–20 content slides)
1. Title (name, supervisor, programme, date).
2. Problem and rationale (1–2): evidence of the problem.
3. Objectives and scope (1).
4. Related work and gap (1–2): comparison table, not a list.
5. Proposed approach (3–5): architecture/method diagram, key design decisions.
6. Evaluation (4–6): setup, main results table/chart, the one or two numbers that matter, error analysis.
7. Contributions and limitations (1): honest, matches the thesis.
8. Conclusions and future work (1).
9. Backup slides (after the end): details likely to be asked about.

Design rules:
- One message per slide, written as the slide title in a full sentence ("Hybrid retrieval raises Recall@5 from 0,71 to 0,82").
- Diagrams and charts instead of paragraphs; at most ~6 short lines of text; font ≥ 20 pt (diagram labels ≥ 14 pt).
- Numbers on slides identical to the thesis; same terms as the thesis.
- Slide numbers (committee will say "slide 12").
- Contrast readable on a projector; no decorative animation.

## 3. Rehearsal
- Write speaker notes, not a script to read. Time it: aim for 90% of the allowed time.
- Rehearse at least twice aloud, once in front of someone; cut, do not speed up.
- Prepare a 1-minute version of the contribution ("elevator pitch") for the closing.
- Check the room setup, file formats (PDF backup), demo video in case a live demo fails.

## 4. Anticipating questions
Prepare short answers (30–60 s) for:
- Why this problem / why this method rather than <alternative>?
- What exactly is your own contribution versus existing tools/libraries?
- How were data collected, cleaned, labelled; is there leakage; how representative?
- Why these baselines and metrics? Are differences significant?
- What happens when <edge case / scale / failure>?
- Limitations; what would you do with 6 more months?
- Practical deployment (đề án): who uses it, cost, maintenance, measured benefit at the unit.
- Every criticism in the written reviews (answer each one, see §5).
- Definitions of every term and acronym on your slides.

## 5. Answering the committee
- Listen to the end; note the question; answer the question asked, briefly, then stop.
- Agree with valid criticism and say how it will be fixed in the revision; do not argue defensively.
- If you do not know: say so, say how you would find out.
- Group several questions from one member and answer in order; refer to backup slides.
- Address reviewer reports point by point: "Về ý kiến thứ nhất của phản biện 1…".

## 6. After the defense
- Get the minutes' list of required and recommended revisions.
- Revise within the school's deadline; write a giải trình chỉnh sửa (table: comment | change made | page) and obtain the confirmation of the council chair (and supervisor if required).
- Lodge the final version in the formats requested (print, PDF); check that page numbers and lists are regenerated.

## 7. Conference talks and seminars
Same structure, compressed: problem (1–2 slides), idea (1), method (2–4), results (2–3), takeaway (1). 12–15 minutes + questions is common; respect the session chair's time signals.
