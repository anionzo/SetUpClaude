# Citation styles

Sources checked against: IEEE Reference Guide (IEEE Publication Operations, version 3.28.2025); NLM "Samples of Formatted References" (ICMJE); Cite Them Right Harvard (Newcastle University guide 2025); Turabian 9th author-date quick guide (derived from Chicago 17); APA 7 (apastyle.apa.org). Items marked [unverified] have not been checked against the original text. School/journal rules always take priority.

## IEEE

### In text
- Number in square brackets, on the text line, before punctuation: "…như trong [4]." One unique number per reference throughout the document; when citing again reuse the old number; no ibid., op. cit.
- Multiple sources: "[2], [4], [5]". Ranges: the IEEE guide for IEEE publications requires separate numbers "[1], [2], [3]"; many schools/theses accept "[1]–[3]". Follow the submission venue's template.
- Author in the sentence: one or two authors give surnames ("Brown and Jones [5]"); three or more authors "Wood et al. [7]" (Vietnamese text: "Wood và cộng sự [7]").
- Location: `[3, p. 5]`, `[3, pp. 5–10]`, `[3, Sec. 4.5]`, `[3, Fig. 1]`, `[3, eq. (2)]`, `[3, Ch. 2]`. In a Vietnamese text, per school rules: `[3, tr. 5]`.

### Reference list
- Number in order of first citation, with the bracketed number left-aligned in a column.
- Names: given-name initials then surname: "J. K. Author". List up to 6 authors; over 6 list the first author + "et al.".
- Article titles in quotation marks, sentence case; journal/book titles in italic; journal and conference names abbreviated per the IEEE abbreviation list (Trans., Proc., Int., Conf., Symp., Commun., Technol., Syst., Univ.…).
- Month abbreviations: Jan., Feb., Mar., Apr., May, Jun., Jul., Aug., Sep., Oct., Nov., Dec.; two months "Jul./Aug. 2015".
- Each entry ends with a period, unless it ends with a URL. DOI in the form "doi: 10.xxxx/…" goes at the end, before the URL if both are present.
- Access date: "Accessed: Feb. 1, 2025. [Online]. Available: URL".

### Samples
```
Tạp chí:  [1] A. B. Tran and C. D. Le, "Title in sentence case," IEEE Trans. Knowl. Data Eng., vol. 35, no. 4, pp. 101–115, Apr. 2023, doi: 10.1109/TKDE.2023.0000000.
Hội nghị: [2] A. B. Tran, "Title," in Proc. Int. Conf. Data Eng. (ICDE), Lyon, France, May 2024, pp. 12–20, doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.
Sách:     [3] A. Author, Title of Book, 2nd ed. City, Country: Publisher, 2020.
Chương:   [4] A. Author, "Chapter title," in Book Title, B. Editor, Ed. City, Country: Publisher, 2019, pp. 55–70.
Luận văn: [5] A. Author, "Title," M.S. thesis, Dept. Comput. Sci., Univ. Name, City, Country, 2024.
Luận án:  [6] A. Author, "Title," Ph.D. dissertation, Dept., Univ., City, Country, 2022.
arXiv:    [7] A. Author and B. Author, "Title," 2023, arXiv:2305.12345.
Web:      [8] Org. Name. "Page title." Site Name. Accessed: Mar. 5, 2025. [Online]. Available: https://...
Dữ liệu:  [9] Author/Org., Year, "Dataset title," Repository, doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.
Chuẩn:    [10] Title of Standard, Standard number, Org., Year.
Sáng chế: [11] A. Author, "Title," U.S. Patent 1 234 567, Jan. 1, 2020.
```
Software: IEEE has no dedicated template; give author, software name, version, release date, repository/address, identifier (DOI if available), following the FORCE11 principles.

## APA 7

### In text
- One author: (Smith, 2020) or Smith (2020). Two authors: (Smith & Jones, 2020); in the sentence "Smith và Jones (2020)". Three or more authors: (Martin et al., 2020) from the first citation; if two shortened citations coincide, add surnames until they are distinguished.
- Multiple sources in one parenthesis: alphabetical, separated by semicolons: (Adams, 2018; Brown, 2020). Same author: (Lê, 2019, 2021).
- Direct quotation: (Smith, 2020, p. 5), (Smith, 2020, pp. 5–6); with no pages, use the paragraph (para. 3) or the section name.
- Organization as author: give it in full; if a long name has an abbreviation, first citation (World Health Organization [WHO], 2021), afterwards (WHO, 2021).
- No date: (Tổ chức, n.d.); a Vietnamese text may use "(Tổ chức, k.n.)" if the school requires.
- Secondary: (Parker, 1978, as cited in Bloom et al., 2017); only Bloom et al. goes into the list.
- Same author same year: 2020a, 2020b (sorted by title).

### Reference list
- Alphabetical by first author's surname; same surname by given-name initial; same author by year; a single-author work comes before multi-author works with the same first author. Hanging indent, not numbered.
- Names: "Surname, A. B."; "&" before the last author; list up to 20 authors; from 21, list the first 19, "…", then the last.
- Article/chapter titles: sentence case, not italic. Books, reports, theses: sentence case, italic. Journal names: capitalize each word, italic; volume italic; issue in parentheses, not italic.
- DOI as URL `https://doi.org/…`; no "Retrieved from" for stable sources; access date only for frequently changing pages.

### Samples
```
Tạp chí:  Tran, A. B., & Le, C. D. (2023). Title in sentence case. Journal of Information Science, 49(3), 45–67. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx
Hội nghị: Tran, A. B. (2024). Title. In C. Editor (Ed.), Proceedings of the ... Conference (pp. 1–10). Publisher. https://doi.org/...
Trình bày: Tran, A. B. (2024, June 10–13). Title [Paper presentation]. Conference Name, City, Country.
Sách:     Author, A. (2020). Title of book (2nd ed.). Publisher.
Chương:   Author, A. (2020). Chapter title. In B. Editor & C. Editor (Eds.), Book title (pp. 115–129). Publisher.
Luận văn: Nguyen, V. A. (2022). Title [Master's thesis, University Name]. Repository. URL
Web:      Organisation. (2021, March 5). Page title. Site Name. https://...
arXiv:    Author, A. (2023). Title. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2305.12345
Dữ liệu:  Author. (2020). Title of dataset (Version 2) [Data set]. Publisher. https://doi.org/...
Phần mềm: Author. (2021). Title (Version 1.2) [Computer software]. Publisher. URL
```
Legal documents in APA 7 follow Bluebook style (chapter 11) [unverified]; in Vietnam they usually follow school rules (see `viet-nam.md`).

## Harvard (Cite Them Right)
Harvard has many variants; the school's guide is the standard.
- In text: (Jones and Henry, 2011, p. 1150) or Evans (2015, p. 33). Up to three authors give all names (Hill, Smith and Jones, 1997); from four authors: Harrison et al. (2003).
- Secondary: "Smith (2018, cited in Jones, 2020, p. 21)"; only Jones goes into the list.
- Same author same year: 2001a, 2001b. Multiple sources: semicolon [unverified]. No date: (n.d.) [unverified].
- Reference list: alphabetical by surname; "Surname, A." joined with "and"; list all authors; year in parentheses with no period after: `(2010) Title.`; article title in single quotation marks ' '; journal name in italic; online: `Available at: URL (Accessed: 3 August 2025).`
```
Tạp chí: Tran, A. and Le, C. (2023) 'Title in sentence case', Journal Name, 22(4), pp. 300–314. Available at: https://doi.org/...
Sách:    Grix, J. and Watkins, G. (2010) Title of book. Publisher.
Chương:  Author, A. (2015) 'Chapter title', in B. Editor and C. Editor (eds.) Book title. Publisher, pp. 133–154.
Luận án: Author, A. (2022) Title. PhD thesis. University. Available at: URL (Accessed: date).
Web:     Author, A. (2023) Page title. Available at: URL (Accessed: 26 March 2025).
```

## Vancouver (NLM/ICMJE)
- In text: Arabic numerals in order of first citation, in square brackets, parentheses or superscript depending on the journal; multiple sources [1,3,5] or [1–3] [unverified, depends on the journal].
- Reference list: names "Halpern SD, Ubel PA" (surname, initials without periods or spaces); first 6 authors + "et al." (NLM now lists all, depending on the journal); article title in sentence case, no quotation marks, not italic; journal name abbreviated per NLM; `Year Month day;volume(issue):pages.` with abbreviated pages (284-7).
- Online: `[Internet]`, `[cited 2025 Mar 5]`, `Available from: URL`.
```
Tạp chí:  Halpern SD, Ubel PA, Caplan AL. Title in sentence case. N Engl J Med. 2002 Jul 25;347(4):284-7.
Sách:     Murray PR, Rosenthal KS. Medical microbiology. 4th ed. St. Louis: Mosby; 2002.
Chương:   Author AA. Chapter title. In: Editor BB, editor. Book title. City: Publisher; 2002. p. 93-113.
Luận án:  Author AA. Title [dissertation]. City (State): University; 2002.
Hội nghị: Author AA. Title. In: Editor BB, editors. Proceedings title; 2002 Apr 3-5; City, Country. City: Publisher; 2002. p. 182-91.
Web:      Site name [Internet]. City: Publisher; c2016 [cited 2016 Dec 27]. Available from: URL
Phần mềm: Author AA. Name. Version 2.3.2 [software]. 2013 Jan 14 [cited 2015 Feb 14]. Available from: URL
```

## Chicago author-date
- In text: (Kitamura 2017, 25): no comma between author and year, comma before the page. Two authors (Sassler and Miller 2017); three authors give all (Keng, Lin, and Orazem 2017); from four: (Weber et al. 2017). No date: (Columbia University n.d.). Same year: 2017a, 2017b [unverified].
- Reference list: alphabetical; the first author is inverted (surname first), later authors are not: "Keng, Shao-Hsun, Chun-Hung Lin, and Peter F. Orazem. 2017."; list up to 10 authors, over 10 list the first 7 + et al. (Chicago 18 changes this [unverified]); article title in quotation marks, books/journals in italic, headline-style capitalization; DOI as URL.
```
Tạp chí: Keng, Shao-Hsun, Chun-Hung Lin, and Peter F. Orazem. 2017. "Title in Headline Style." Journal of Human Capital 11, no. 1: 1–34. https://doi.org/...
Sách:    Kitamura, Katie. 2017. A Separation. New York: Riverhead Books.
Chương:  Author, First. 2016. "Chapter Title." In Book Title, edited by First Editor, 19–56. City: Publisher.
Luận án: Author, First. 2016. "Title." PhD diss., University Name.
Web:     Organisation. n.d. "Page Title." Accessed May 15, 2017. URL.
```
Chicago notes-bibliography (footnotes), used in history and the humanities, is not covered here; see the Chicago Manual of Style.
